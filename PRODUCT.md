# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, prospective collaborators, and clients evaluating Nasif Fuad for applied AI, automation, growth, go-to-market, revenue operations, analytics, and technical business development work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes Nasif's cross-functional value legible: connecting technical systems, commercial execution, and business analysis to measurable business outcomes. Success means a visitor can quickly understand what Nasif does, where he can contribute, and how to start a conversation.

## Positioning

Nasif is positioned as an **Applied AI & Growth Specialist** who connects AI, data, go-to-market systems, and execution rather than presenting those capabilities as isolated disciplines.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate selected systems, capabilities, working methods, current projects, target roles, and contact options on a single-page portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Astro as a static website.
- The portfolio must remain responsive and accessible across desktop and mobile web.
- Role and capability claims must stay grounded in supplied copy and visible project evidence.
- The primary contact method is email.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Nasif Fuad.
- Canonical role label: Applied AI & Growth Specialist.
- Voice: direct, business-driven, practical, and focused on outcomes.
- Existing cobalt, near-black, white, and neutral visual identity remains authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current portfolio copy, selected systems, capability descriptions, project imagery, and role positioning. No testimonials, client logos, or quantified commercial results are currently supplied, so future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lead with business problems and outcomes, then explain the technical mechanism.
- Make cross-functional breadth feel connected rather than scattered.
- Prefer specific systems and workflows over abstract AI claims.
- Keep the next action clear for employers, collaborators, and clients.
