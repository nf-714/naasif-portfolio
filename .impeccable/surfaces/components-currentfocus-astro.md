# Current focus extension

Scope: A new current-work section on the existing home page, placed immediately before the CTA. Visitor mode: Read and persuade.

Audience and job: Prospective clients, collaborators, and peers who want a concrete view of what Nasif is actively learning and building. The section should communicate range without making unfinished products sound launched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show five active areas clearly and honestly. Refuse decorative structure; let a simple ruled list carry the sequence.

OWN-WORLD: The established near-black field, off-white type, hairline rules, characterful display face, and electric cobalt accent remain unchanged.

STORY: The visitor reads one direct heading, then scans from machine-learning study through commercial tools and Filo AI, with each item described honestly as learning, building, or developing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The established two-column section header leads into five flat horizontal entries with cobalt numerals, concise titles, short descriptions, and hairline separators.

FORM: User-requested local extension, simplified from the generated reference at `.impeccable/mocks/current-focus-reference.png`. Motion uses the site’s existing staggered reveal system without adding another decorative interaction.

FINISH: Responsive at desktop and mobile, reduced-motion safe, build verified, and checked against the incumbent page system.
